FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find your answer? Just write to me, I'm happy to help.

What language are the sessions in?

English or Hungarian. With mixed, international teams we can use both, whatever works best for the group.

Do you work online or in person?

Both. Online from anywhere, in person in and around Budapest. Other locations by arrangement.

How much does a programme cost?

Every programme is tailored, so there's no fixed price list. After the free discovery call I send a detailed written proposal.

How long does a coaching process take?

It depends on the goal. We agree on the number and frequency of sessions together in the first conversation.

What's the difference between team building and team coaching?

Team building is a shared experience and connection, usually one or two days. Team coaching is a longer process that works on specific blockers in how the team operates.

Do participants need agile experience?

No. I adapt the agile tools to the team's level, from complete beginners to experienced Scrum teams.
